Returns, Cancellations & Refunds

Last updated: 10 October 2026

We want you to be happy with your order. This page explains how cancellations, returns and refunds work.

Cancelling an order

  • Order placed, not yet packed: cancel from your account (My orders → Cancel order). It is cancelled straight away.
  • Already packed: send a cancel request from your account and we will try to stop the parcel and confirm.
  • Already shipped: it cannot be cancelled, but you can request a return after it is delivered.

Returning an item

You can request a return within 7 days of delivery if the item is unused, unworn and in its original condition with tags and packaging.

ItemReturnable?
Clothing, footwear, bags, wallets, belts, watches, sunglasses, caps, scarves, phone cases and keychainsYes, within 7 days, unused and in original condition
Earrings, hair clips, claw clips, hair bands, scrunchies, rings and other items worn on the body or in the hairOnly if damaged, defective or the wrong item, because of hygiene
Items that are used, washed, altered or missing tagsNo

Damaged, defective or wrong items

If something arrives damaged, defective or different from what you ordered, tell us within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and clear photos. We will replace it or refund you.

How to ask for a return or refund

  1. Log in and open your account (your name at the top of the store).
  2. Find the order and choose Return / refund (available once the order shows as Delivered).
  3. Pick a reason and add details. You can follow the status in your account.

You can also raise a ticket, email threadly.care.tn@gmail.com or call 8072796630.

Refunds

  • Paid online: refunded to the original payment method (UPI, card, netbanking or wallet) within 5-7 working days after the refund is approved. Your bank may take a little longer to show it.
  • Cash on delivery: refunded by UPI or bank transfer. We will ask for your details.
  • Delivery fee: refunded only if the return is because of our mistake (wrong, damaged or defective item) or the order is cancelled before it ships.
  • Coupons: the discount is adjusted in the refund amount.

Need help?

Call 8072796630, email threadly.care.tn@gmail.com or raise a ticket. Please quote your order number.
