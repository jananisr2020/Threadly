Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This policy explains how Threadly ("Threadly", "we", "us") handles your personal information when you browse, create an account or place an order on this website. By using the site you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the site.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and a password. Your password is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form, so nobody at Threadly can read it.
  • Order and delivery details: name, mobile number, delivery address and pincode, the items you order, the amount, coupon used, payment method and payment status.
  • Payment details: card, UPI and bank details are entered in Razorpay's secure window and are never stored by us. We keep only the payment reference and status Razorpay gives us.
  • Support tickets, returns and reviews: the messages, order numbers and ratings you send us.
  • Technical information: your device and browser type and IP address, collected in the hosting provider's server logs, and the browser storage described in our Cookie Policy.

2. How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and log you in.
  • To process payments, pack, ship and deliver your orders, and handle cancellations, returns and refunds.
  • To send order, ticket and password-reset emails.
  • To answer your questions and complaints.
  • To show reviews, keep the store secure and prevent fraud or misuse.
  • To meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We use your information with your consent and where needed to fulfil your order or comply with the law. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Cookies and browser storage

We use essential browser storage for your cart, wishlist and login, and optional storage for convenience features that you can accept or decline. Details and your choices are in the Cookie Policy.

5. How long we keep it

We keep account details while your account is open. Order, payment and delivery records are kept for as long as needed for accounting, tax and legal purposes and to handle returns, refunds and disputes. Support tickets are kept for as long as needed to resolve your issue and to keep a record of it. You can ask us to delete your account at any time (see section 7), and we will delete what the law does not require us to keep.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information: passwords are hashed, the site uses HTTPS, access to our order records is limited to authorised staff, and payments are handled by Razorpay. No online service is completely secure, so please use a strong, unique password and keep it private.

7. Your rights and choices

You can ask us to show you the personal information we hold, correct it, delete it, or stop using it for a purpose you agreed to. You can withdraw cookie consent at any time using "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page. To make a request, email threadly.care.tn@gmail.com, call 8072796630, or raise a ticket. We may need to confirm your identity first.

8. Children

Threadly is meant for adults. If you are under 18, please use the site only with a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect information from children without a guardian's involvement.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact and grievances

For privacy questions or complaints write to threadly.care.tn@gmail.com or call 8072796630. Please include your name and, if relevant, your order number.
