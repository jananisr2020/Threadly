Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This page explains where we deliver, how long it takes, what it costs and how you can follow your order.

Where we deliver

We currently deliver across Tamil Nadu, including Coimbatore, Chennai, Madurai, Salem, Tiruchirappalli, Tirunelveli, Erode, Tiruppur, Vellore and all other cities and towns. We are expanding to other states soon. Type your pincode or area name in the box at the top of the store to check your area and see the expected delivery date. Orders to pincodes outside Tamil Nadu cannot be placed yet.

Delivery time

AreaEstimated delivery
Chennai and Coimbatore2-3 days after dispatch
Other places in Tamil Nadu3-5 days after dispatch

We pack and hand over orders to the delivery partner within 1-2 working days. Remote areas, public holidays, bad weather and courier disruption can add time. Dates shown on the site are estimates, not guarantees.

Delivery charges

  • Free on orders of ₹299 and above (after discounts).
  • ₹39 on orders below ₹299.

The exact amount is shown in your cart and at checkout before you pay.

Payment methods

Juno currently accepts prepaid payments through Razorpay using UPI, cards and netbanking. Cash on delivery is not available. Your payment method and final total are shown at checkout before you pay.

Tracking your order

When your order ships, the tracking number appears next to it in your account (your name at the top of the store → your orders). You will also see the order status change from New to Packed, Shipped and Delivered.

If delivery fails

If the delivery partner cannot reach you, they may try again or contact you, so please keep your mobile number and address correct. If a parcel is lost or very late, tell us and we will either re-send it or refund you. Please check the parcel on arrival and tell us within 48 hours if it is damaged.
