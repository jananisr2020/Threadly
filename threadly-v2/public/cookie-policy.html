Cookie Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This policy explains what browser storage the Juno site uses. Juno does not use advertising cookies or tracking cookies, and does not run analytics that follow you around the web.

Your choice

When you first visit, a banner lets you choose Accept all or Essential only. You can change your mind at any time with Cookie settings, which is also in the footer of the store. Choosing Essential only removes the optional items below from your device.

Essential storage (always on)

These make the shop work, so they cannot be switched off here. Most are held in your browser's local storage rather than as cookies.

WhatPurposeHow long
Login sessionKeeps you logged inUntil you log out or clear your browser data
Cart, saved-for-later, couponRemembers what you put in your bagUntil you check out or clear your browser data
WishlistRemembers items you heartedUntil you clear your browser data
Your cookie choiceRemembers whether you accepted, so we do not ask againUntil you clear your browser data
Razorpay payment windowSecure payment and fraud prevention. Loaded only when you press Pay, and it may set its own cookiesAs set by Razorpay

Optional storage (only if you choose Accept all)

WhatPurposeHow long
Recent searchesShows your last searches in the search boxUntil you clear your browser data
Recently viewed productsShows the "Recently viewed" row on the home pageUntil you clear your browser data
Delivery pincodeRemembers your pincode so delivery dates are readyUntil you clear your browser data

If you choose Essential only, these still work during your visit but are forgotten when you close the page.

Third parties

  • Google Fonts supplies the site's typeface. When the page loads, your browser contacts Google, which receives your IP address and browser details.
  • Razorpay is loaded only at payment, as described above.

Managing storage in your browser

You can delete cookies and site data in your browser settings at any time. If you do, you will be logged out and your cart and wishlist will be emptied.
